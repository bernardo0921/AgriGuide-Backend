Getting Started

Welcome to AgriGuide! Here are a few tips to get you started:

  • AI Chat: Ask any farming-related question to our AI assistant and get instant advice.
  • Community: Share your knowledge, ask questions, and connect with other farmers.
  • Tutorials: Browse our library of articles and videos to learn new skills.
  • Daily Tip: Check the home screen for a new AI-powered farming tip every day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I change my profile information?

A: You can update your profile information by navigating to the "Profile" section in the app menu and tapping the "Edit Profile" button.

Q: How do I post in the community?

A: Go to the "Community" tab and tap the "+" icon to create a new post. You can add text and images to share with the community.
